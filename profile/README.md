# Cadence - Tempo Tracker for Musicians

## About Cadence

Cadence is a focused tempo and rhythm workspace for musicians who want to measure, practice, and improve timing. Cadence keeps tempo tracking readable, rhythm practice steady, and daily sessions easier for guitarists, drummers, pianists, producers, and students who rely on Cadence for clear tempo insight. The Cadence tempo tracker turns raw timing into a calm map, so you can see where your rhythm drifts and where it locks in.

Cadence turns raw timing into a clear map. With tempo tracker and rhythm practice workflows in mind, musicians can tap, sort, and plan sessions with less guesswork than a standalone metronome alone.

Cadence keeps the process simple. Choose a tempo or tap along, run a Cadence tempo tracker pass, review beat charts and sortable lists, then decide what to repeat and what to push faster. The Cadence metronome tool approach favors readable panels, predictable controls, and a steady tempo report view for home players, students, teachers, and touring musicians.

In longer sessions, Cadence connects exploration and action. Players move from tap to comparison, then to a finished tempo overview without restarting other tools. That matters when setlists, click tracks, or rehearsal patterns change quickly and a simple beat counter is not enough for confident decisions.

Cadence also fits common practice expectations. People checking BPM counter or beat trainer usually want stable timing, clear units, and a calm path from first launch to a useful tempo analyzer result on everyday hardware.
